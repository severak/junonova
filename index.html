Simplified hands on control panel for Novation Mininova.

Select patch you want to edit, press INIT PATCH and program it like it's Roland Juno 60. Press SAVE on Mininova itself to save preset you just created.

Warning! It will happily rewrite you patches and produce some screeching sounds. Also it sometimes glitches while editing sound.

This is in progress. Sections and controls which are crossed over are not yet implemented. Synchronization from device to computer also not implemented.
